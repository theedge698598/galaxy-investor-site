00

Investment thesis: own the scarce assets that matter.

The Edge framework favors concentration, structural shifts, and disciplined value capture over broad market mimicry.

We believe the biggest long-term returns rarely come from owning everything.

They come from identifying a small number of structural shifts early, understanding where value is likely to concentrate, and owning meaningful positions in the assets most exposed to those changes. The goal is not to look diversified for its own sake. The goal is to be right about a handful of important shifts that can matter for years.

If this trend becomes real, who gets paid repeatedly?
01 · Structural shift

Find transitions that can matter for years.

We look for technology, digital finance, and infrastructure transitions where new systems are replacing old ones over a five-to-fifteen-year horizon.

02 · Control point

Focus on rails, bottlenecks, and platforms.

We prefer assets positioned where demand, usage, trust, or infrastructure dependency can compound: compute layers, settlement rails, custody, workflow systems, distribution, and scarce capacity.

03 · Value capture

Require economics, not just narrative.

A thesis must explain how the asset makes money if the trend wins, why economics may endure, and why customers, developers, institutions, or counterparties may depend on it over time.

Concentration is not gambling. It has to be earned.

Volatility alone is not a reason to sell. A broken thesis is. We watch for thesis decay, failed execution, competitive pressure, broken economics, valuation excess, and market-structure changes. When the reason for owning something no longer holds, the position must be reviewed, reduced, or exited.

01

Methodology: rank the evidence, not the narrative.

Scores are qualitative research scores, not price targets or investment recommendations.

Scoring model

  • Signal: quality of evidence, customer proof, and seriousness of the thesis.
  • Asymmetry: upside versus current market understanding and narrative saturation.
  • Durability: likelihood the advantage can matter over a 5-20 year horizon.
  • Total: simple sum of Signal + Asymmetry + Durability.

Operating posture

Prefer broad, serious businesses with control points: workflow, governed data, memory, edge inference, custom silicon, networking, and infrastructure bottlenecks. Avoid promotion-heavy names where the story is clearer than the monetization logic.
