Operating thesis

The AI stack is becoming physical. Models get attention, but the durable economics may accrue to constrained infrastructure: power, cooling, interconnect, data-center campuses, chips, deployment tooling, and trusted enterprise workflows. The Edge lens: follow the bottleneck, then ask who can monetize it without heroic assumptions.

What to watch now

  • New committed power, not vague campus capacity.
  • Customer concentration and contract quality.
  • Inference demand moving from demos into production workloads.
  • Gross margin durability as GPU supply normalizes.
