Featured primer · GLXY

Galaxy Digital is more than a crypto price proxy.

The cleaner long-term question is whether Galaxy becomes a core institutional platform for digital asset markets, tokenization, custody, trading, and infrastructure.

Framework

The research lens.

Crypto coverage has to avoid both hype and dismissal. The useful question is who owns real rails if digital markets mature.

Institutional rails

Trading, lending, asset management, custody, and market structure for larger pools of capital.

Tokenization

Potential demand for financial assets moving onto digital rails if regulation and adoption improve.

Helios / infrastructure

Optionality tied to compute and infrastructure, which needs proof before being fully valued.

Bull case

Galaxy becomes a scarce public-market crypto infrastructure name.

If institutions keep adopting digital asset rails, Galaxy may deserve to be valued as more than a volatile crypto P&L proxy.

Bear case

The business remains too cyclical and crypto-price dependent.

If earnings stay tied mostly to market cycles and token prices, the rerating case weakens.
