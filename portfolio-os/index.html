What it does

An investing app for the decision after the trade.

Your brokerage can show price. Portfolio OS is built for the harder question: is the reason you bought still valid?

01 / Track

Holdings with context.

Record what you own, approximate weight, conviction, risk bucket, thesis, catalysts, and kill criteria without turning investing into homework.

Portfolio command center
02 / Think

Thesis health.

Separate company-level conviction from theme-level evidence. Track whether new information strengthens, weakens, or changes the original reason for owning.

Theme + holding dashboards
03 / Review

Better sell decisions.

Review sales using missed gains, avoided losses, redeployment, and the original intent — not just hindsight shame.

Intent-aware analytics
04 / Journal

Capture why fast.

Use quick reason tags when buying or selling, then add deeper rationale later from the holding drawer or transaction detail.

Low-friction decision journal
05 / Review

Prompted thesis reviews.

Use structured prompts to revisit the original thesis, surface missing risks, and define what would make you add, hold, trim, or exit.

Launch core feature
06 / Roadmap

AI comes after the workflow works.

AI assistance is a planned layer: thesis summaries, pushback, and weekly review once the core decision records are strong enough to make it useful.

Planned later, not launch promise
Who it is for

Built for serious retail investors, not spreadsheet tourists.

Portfolio OS is for people who want to improve their process. It works best for investors who own individual stocks, crypto, ETFs, concentrated positions, or thematic bets — and want a clear record of why.

Who it is not for.

It is not a get-rich-quick app, signal service, brokerage, or AI stock picker. If someone only wants “what should I buy today?” this is the wrong product.

Concentrated investor

You own fewer names with real conviction.

You need thesis tracking, position review, and failure conditions — not generic diversification lectures.

Thesis builder

You invest around big ideas.

AI agents, edge compute, tokenization, stablecoins, power infrastructure — Portfolio OS lets you track themes and the companies capturing them.

Self-aware trader

You want to learn from buys and sells.

The app helps you see patterns: panic selling, FOMO adds, risk-reduction trims, missed re-entry rules, and thesis drift.

Retail pro

You want a fund-like process without fund complexity.

Simple enough for real life, serious enough to make better decisions over time.

How it works

From trade to thesis review.

Portfolio OS should feel lighter than a spreadsheet and more thoughtful than a brokerage dashboard. The beta should prove the manual workflow before adding heavy automation.

Step 01

Add holdings.

Start manually or from import. The beta should not require brokerage login.

Step 02

Capture intent.

Use quick reason tags and optional rationale fields for thesis, risk, catalyst, and re-buy condition.

Step 03

Review changes.

Use recurring prompts and evidence notes to decide whether the thesis is stronger, weaker, or unchanged.

Step 04

Improve decisions.

Learn whether buys, sells, trims, and redeployments are improving your process over time.

Private beta

Help build the decision layer for retail investors.

The first beta should be small: thoughtful investors, honest feedback, real workflows, and no noise. The goal is not downloads yet. The goal is whether people come back because the core tracking and review system makes them better.
