Features · Decision quality

A portfolio app for the decisions behind the numbers.

Edge Portfolio keeps the normal portfolio math, then adds the missing layer: why the decision was made, how it aged, and what should be reviewed next.

01

Dashboard, pricing, cash, and benchmark context.

See total value, cash, unrealized gains and losses, realized sales performance, live pricing with CAD conversion, and S&P 500 comparison in one calm view.

  • VALUETotal portfolio value, account cash, unrealized P/L, and realized sales performance.
  • PRICINGLive price updates with currency normalization for Canadian investors.
  • COMPAREBenchmark scoreboard so indexing remains the honest alternative.

Sales tracking that asks the uncomfortable question.

Practical · Product scope

Serious, private, and intentionally not advice.

Edge Portfolio is a decision-quality layer. It does not place trades, recommend securities, manage assets, or pretend hindsight is the same thing as process.

What it is

Operating system.

A private place to track holdings, transactions, thesis notes, sales, redeployment, benchmark context, review prompts, and audit history.

  • Portfolio dashboard and live pricing with CAD conversion
  • Decision Journal and transaction ledger
  • Sales tracking and redeployment planner
  • Authentication, persistent storage, mobile-friendly workflows, and production QA tooling
What it is not

Not a brokerage.

Edge Portfolio helps investors evaluate their own process. It is not built to tell users what to buy, execute trades, or replace financial judgment.

  • No trade execution
  • No stock-picking promises
  • No financial advice
  • No gamified alerts designed to increase activity
Last word

Become a better decision-maker, not just a better spreadsheet maintainer.

Edge Portfolio is for investors who want to understand their own behavior: why they bought, when their thesis changed, whether selling helped, whether cash was redeployed well, and whether they are beating the simple alternative of holding or indexing.
