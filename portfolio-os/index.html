Features · 01–04

The four moves every operator makes.

Portfolio OS organizes the work of running a real, concentrated portfolio. Less dashboard, more cockpit — the surfaces map to what you actually do on a Wednesday afternoon.

01

Every position carries a written thesis.

Conviction without writing is a hunch. The journal turns each holding into a structured argument: thesis TLDR, key risk, bear case, kill criteria, next catalyst, last reviewed.

  • TLDRTwo sentences. If you can't write the thesis short, you don't have one.
  • KILLThe specific number, event, or print that ends the position. Stated before the trade.
  • REVIEW90-day timer per holding. Skipped reviews surface as signals.

02

Trade in your own words.

Type a sentence. The parser extracts action, ticker, shares, price, currency, FX, theme, conviction, and thesis — then shows you the draft before anything touches the book.

  • PARSELocal NL parser. Never sends your moves to a third party.
  • DRAFTConfidence score per field. Missing fields surface — they don't get guessed.
  • UNDOEvery applied command takes a snapshot. Undo restores the exact pre-apply state.

03

Score every sale 30, 90, and 180 days out.

A sale isn't a decision until you know whether you were right. Sale quality tracks each exit against price action and the alternative — what you redeployed into — over three windows.

  • 30DQuick read: did the stock fall, climb, or stay flat after the sale?
  • 90DReal signal: noise has cleared; the decision starts to grade.
  • 180DVerdict: win, wrong-way, or too-early. Plus what the redeployment did.

04

Pair every sale to its destination.

Selling without buying isn't a position — it's a forecast of cash. Redeployment links every exit to where the proceeds went, then grades the rotation against holding the original.

  • LINKTag the dollars from a sale to one or more buys. Partial fills welcome.
  • COMPARE"You sold A and bought B. Here's what that decision returned over 90 days."
  • PATIENCECash counts as a destination. Patient capital is logged as a decision, not absence of one.
