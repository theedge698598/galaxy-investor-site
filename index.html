What The Edge is

A research system for knowing why you own it.

The homepage sets the philosophy. The research library holds the individual work: Galaxy, Qualcomm, the AI Leaderboard, and future primers.

01

Find structural shifts.

We look for transitions that can matter for years: AI infrastructure, on-device compute, tokenized finance, scarce rails, and market structure changes.

02

Understand the business.

The idea has to connect to revenue, margins, pricing power, distribution, bottlenecks, or durable strategic position.

03

Track what breaks it.

Every thesis needs risks, watch items, and explicit reasons to review, reduce, or exit. Conviction without review becomes stubbornness.
